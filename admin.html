ℹ️ How It Works

📌 Both files must be open in the same browser — content flows from admin.html → localStorage → index.html automatically. No server needed.

📷 Images are stored as Base64 inside localStorage. The total limit is ~5MB per domain. Keep images small (compress before upload) or use fewer photos.

🔄 After uploading, open or refresh index.html in a new tab. Go to Gallery or Patient Education — content will appear immediately.
