Our Story

About Shree Vinayak Hospital

Shree Vinayak Hospital is a doctor-owned and doctor-run multispecialty hospital at 347, Mandakini Colony, B-Sector, Kolar Road, Bhopal – 462042. Founded in 2018, it is built on a simple mission: bring advanced orthopaedic and spine care to the people of Madhya Pradesh.

The hospital specialises in spine surgeries (including minimally invasive endoscopic / keyhole approaches), orthopaedic surgeries, gynaecology and obstetrics, reconstructive plastic surgeries, and general medicine.

Currently at 55-bed capacity across deluxe, semi-deluxe, and general ward configurations, with a fully equipped OT featuring advanced endoscopic systems.

Specialists include Dr. Mukul Gupta (19+ yrs, Spine & Ortho), Dr. Aditi Singhal (9+ yrs, Gynaecology), Dr. Vikas Malviya (General Medicine), and visiting specialists in multiple domains.
